# Teaching Materials for Predictive Modeling with Decision Trees

This repository contains a consolidated collection of German-language teaching materials developed in the context of research on teaching predictive modeling with data-based decision trees in secondary education at Paderborn University.

## Contents

The materials are organized into nine instructional phases:

1. Recommendation systems on online platforms
2. Constructing one-level decision trees with data cards
3. Constructing one-level decision trees with CODAP
4. Constructing multi-level decision trees with CODAP
5. Constructing decision trees by acting as an algorithm
6. Evaluating decision trees using test data
7. Evaluating algorithm variants: overfitting and pruning
8. Investigating selection bias in training data
9. Context-sensitive evaluation of classifiers

The collection includes lesson plans, worksheets, presentation slides, teacher guidance, Jupyter notebooks, and data files. Materials are provided in their original German language.

## Research context and scope

These files document a consolidated collection of instructional resources associated with the research project. They should not be interpreted as an exact historical record of the materials used unchanged in every classroom implementation or study.

The materials are shared for research transparency and to support inspection of the instructional design. No guarantee is made that software-dependent activities will run unchanged in current environments.
